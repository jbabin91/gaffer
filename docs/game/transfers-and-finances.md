# Transfers and finances

## Budgets

- A club has a **transfer budget** (for fees) and a **wage budget** (ongoing
  salaries). The board may allow shifting money between them.
- Spending is constrained by these budgets and the club's financial health.

## Transfer windows

- Two windows per season: a summer window and a winter (January) window.
- Outside a window you generally cannot complete permanent signings.

## Deal types

- Permanent transfers (a fee), loans (with or without an option/obligation to
  buy), and free transfers (expiring contracts).
- Sales and loans-out free up squad space and wages.

## Value and wages

- Each player has a market value (fee guidance) and a wage.
- These drive squad cost, contract-expiry planning, and transfer decisions.

## What the app tracks today

Per-player market value and wage fields exist on the schema and snapshots.
Transfer windows, budgets, and deal tracking are anticipated domain concepts
without dedicated UI yet.
