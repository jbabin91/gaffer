# Idea 0007: Finances and budgets

Status: exploring
Date: 2026-06-13

## What

Track a career's transfer budget and wage budget, with spent / remaining / net
math — modeled on the budget totals in the transfer-plan sheets (transfer budget,
spent, remaining; wage budget vs wages added). A lead post-platform feature.

## Why

Every transfer plan was costed against the two budgets. Without it, the planning
features can't answer "can I afford this?".

## Open questions

- Per-season budgets (they reset each window) vs a running career ledger?
- Manual budget entry, or derived from recorded transfers?
