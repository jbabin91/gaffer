# Idea 0005: Scouting shortlist (transfer targets)

Status: exploring
Date: 2026-06-13

## What

A per-user, per-career list of players to watch or sign later, modeled on the
"Transfer Targets" tabs in the spreadsheet. Private — career mode is
single-player. Fields beyond a bookmark: tier (A/B/C), gap (the position need it
fills), a sign-status pipeline (target → fee agreed → signed), source/provenance
(`Src`), and notes. References the shared catalog, never another user's squad.

## Why

Targets are where most of the planning happens — tiered, position-mapped, and
tracked through to signing. The user maintained this by hand across several
spreadsheet tabs.

## Open questions

- Tie targets to a career (likely) and/or to a season window?
- Reuse the catalog row, or capture the target's values at add-time (they drift)?
