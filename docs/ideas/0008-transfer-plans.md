# Idea 0008: Transfer plans (windows)

Status: exploring
Date: 2026-06-13

## What

Sequenced window plans — ordered offload → sign steps with budget impact and
done-markers — modeled on the "Summer Plan" and "Suggested First Window" sheets.
Ties targets (`0005`) to finances (`0007`): each step moves a player out or in
and updates the remaining budget.

## Why

The user planned each window as an ordered list (sell these, free this wage, sign
these, net cost) before acting in-game. This is the "planned transfers" half of
the vision.

## Open questions

- A draft plan that can be "applied" to the squad, or just a planning view?
- Depends on `0005` (targets) and `0007` (finances).
