# Idea 0004: Contract, wage, and form entry

Status: exploring
Date: 2026-06-13

## What

Add fields for contract expiry, wage, and market value when adding or editing a
player, and a form (0–10) field when recording a snapshot.

## Why

These columns already exist on the schema and are accepted by the input types —
contract expiry is even shown on the player detail screen — but the forms don't
let you enter them. Contract and wage tracking is central to squad planning.

## Open questions

- Should expiry-soon contracts be highlighted in the squad list?
- Visualize form alongside overall/potential on the development chart?
